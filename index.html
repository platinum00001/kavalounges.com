About Kava Lounges

1

The vibe

Low lights, comfortable seats, board games, acoustic music and conversation. Think coffeehouse meets island porch.

2

How a kava bowl works

Kava is mixed in a shared bowl and served in small cups. In Fiji you clap once, say "Bula!" and drink. Part of the fun is the ritual.

3

Pop-ups first

Before any permanent spot, we'd like to start with pop-up nights at partner venues. If you run a space on the Cape, get in touch through the form.

Kava is a traditional ceremonial and social drink from the Pacific Islands. Cape Kava shares culture, food and hospitality only and makes no health or medical claims. Enjoy responsibly, 21+ where required by local law, and never drive after drinking kava.
